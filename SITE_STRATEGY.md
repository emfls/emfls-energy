# Site Strategy

- Site No.: 18
- Repo: `emfls-energy`
- Domain: `https://energy.emfls.com/`
- Type: Household Energy Guide + Calculators

## Mission

Help households understand where electricity, gas, heating, and cooling energy is used,
and how power, runtime, energy use, and cost estimates relate.

## Topic boundary

Include household energy use, kWh, appliance consumption, standby power, heating/cooling
efficiency, seasonal usage, simple estimates, and safe everyday actions.

Do not provide home-repair instructions, smart-home automation, national industrial policy
analysis, investment advice, current utility rates without dated official evidence, or
guaranteed savings.

## Foundation-first scope

Current scope is the minimum unique site, trust pages, technical SEO, production delivery,
and search registration. Deep guide collections, device libraries, calculators, and the
energy-audit checklist follow the 100-site Foundation/Search Launch pass.

The homepage provides a useful unit explanation and one clearly bounded W-to-kWh example
without presenting it as real-time or measured usage.
