# Design System

## Site identity

An energy monitor for household use: quiet, legible, and grounded in units and assumptions.
This is not a vehicle dashboard or an imitation of another EMFLS site.

## Visual language

- Off-white surfaces with deep green and blue structure.
- Amber is reserved for a small note or caution.
- Use clear editorial typography, generous reading width, and restrained bordered panels.
- Unit formulas can be shown as text; do not draw fake meters, live charts, or data visualizations.
- Do not use unsupported statistics, fabricated product imagery, or fabricated real-time data.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- The header and content reflow without horizontal scrolling at 320 px and wider.
- Keep visible keyboard focus and a skip link to the main content.
- Use semantic landmarks and one primary heading per page.
- Preserve readable line lengths and contrast on the light surfaces.

## Interaction

The Foundation homepage is informational. Calculators and advanced tools are deferred; do
not ship controls that look interactive but have no behavior.
